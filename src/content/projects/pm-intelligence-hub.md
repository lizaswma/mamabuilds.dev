## Why I built this

This was actually the first app I ever built - and the one I still use every single day.

Between meetings and a full inbox, my workday doesn't leave much room for reading the news.
The only pocket of time I reliably get is my commute. And with so much happening in AI week
to week, I kept feeling behind - like I needed to be reading five newsletters just to keep up,
except I never had time to read any of them.

So I built the thing I actually wanted: something that fetches the day's AI headlines for me,
pulls out what matters, and turns it into a short podcast episode I can listen to on my drive
instead of read. And because I'm a PM, not a reporter, I didn't just want the news - I wanted
it translated into "here's what this actually means for how I do my job."

## What it does

- Fetches the day's AI headlines every morning from a range of sources.
- Aggregates and summarizes them - with citations, so I can go read the full article later if
  something's worth digging into.
- Turns the summary into a short daily podcast episode, built for a commute-length listen.
- Frames every story through a PM lens: not just what happened, but what it means for how
  product managers should be thinking about their roadmaps, their tools, and their jobs.
